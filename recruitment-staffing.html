  • Tell us your needs — Chat with our hiring consultants about the role, stack, and team fit you're looking for.
  • Interview shortlisted developers — We match candidates from our talent pool. You interview and assess cultural fit.
  • Hire effortlessly — Once you decide, your engagement manager handles all paperwork and payment processing.

We support permanent full-time, contract, contract-to-hire (C2H), and part-time roles. VGreenTEK manages payroll and logistics. If you wish to convert a contract resource to a full-time hire, a C2H fee applies. We can also help you build entire remote teams.

  • Permanent roles — Fee per hire based on annual salary, with a 3-month replacement guarantee.
  • Part-time — Billed based on logged hours via VGreenTEK's dashboard (or your preferred tracking tool).
  • Contracts (Monthly Retainer) — A fixed monthly cost per developer covering salary, benefits, and admin margin. Ideal for long-term team integration with predictable costs.

For permanent roles, each candidate sets their own salary expectation — your consultant ensures shortlisted profiles meet your budget. For contract roles, you pay for hours logged and approved.
