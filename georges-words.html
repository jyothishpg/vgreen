FOUNDER’S VOICE

In George's words

Co-founder & CEO — on talent, trust, and building VGreenTEK to create lasting value.

In Georgiy’s words

Our philosophy and approach are shaped by experience and belief in people.

01 — Problem

What problem in the world frustrates you the most?

The disconnect between talented people seeking opportunities and organisations struggling to find the right talent. Both sides have what the other needs, yet barriers in the hiring process often prevent the right connections from being made.

02 — Belief

What belief do you hold that most people disagree with? I believe hiring is still fundamentally a people business, despite the rise of AI and automation. While technology can improve efficiency, strong relationships, trust, and human judgment remain the most important factors in making successful placements

03 — Origin

Why start VGreenTEK instead of joining something else? I saw a gap between great talent and the right opportunities, and I wanted to build a company focused on creating lasting value for both clients and candidates. Rather than joining an existing firm, I wanted the freedom to build a business driven by trust, quality, and long-term relationships.

04 — Values

What principles do you never compromise on? Integrity, transparency, and doing what is right for both our clients and candidates. Long-term trust is far more valuable than any short-term gain. Every difficult decision comes back to this.

05 — Vision

What does success look like beyond revenue? Creating meaningful career opportunities, helping businesses build strong teams, and earning the trust of our clients and candidates. If VGreenTEK is making a positive impact on people's lives and becoming a trusted partner, that is true success.

06 — Learning

A failure that fundamentally changed how you lead? Believing that speed alone drives success. I learned that taking the time to listen, communicate clearly, and build the right team leads to better long-term results than rushing decisions. That patience has made me a better leader.

07 — To clients

One thing you'd tell every customer?Great talent is not just a resource — it's a competitive advantage. Investing in the right people and building strong partnerships creates long-term success for any organisation. That's what we're here to help you do.

08 — Culture

What do you want employees to say in five years? That working with me helped them grow professionally and personally, challenged them to reach their potential, and made them feel valued, supported, and respected throughout their journey..

09 — Influence

What has shaped your thinking most? More than any single book or leader, my entrepreneurial journey, the people I've worked with, and lessons from both successes and setbacks. Building businesses, supporting clients, and balancing leadership with family have taught me the importance of resilience, integrity, and continuous learning.

10 — The future

What principles do you never compromise on? Integrity, transparency, and doing what is right for both our clients and candidates. Long-term trust is far more valuable than any short-term gain. Every difficult decision comes back to this.

"The future will belong to people and companies that embrace change, continuously learn, and build meaningful relationships while staying true to their values."

— George Tomy, Co-founder & CEO, VGreenTEK
