OUR INTERVENTION

  • Recruiting for AI, Robotics, and Autonomous Navigation roles requires more than conventional hiring expertise. These positions demand a deep understanding of specialized technologies, emerging skill sets, and a limited talent pool.
  • VGreenTEK partnered with RoshAI to identify and attract highly skilled professionals capable of contributing to advanced AI and robotics initiatives.

  • By combining technical understanding with targeted talent acquisition, VGreenTEK has helped RoshAI build critical capabilities in one of the most competitive hiring landscapes in technology.
