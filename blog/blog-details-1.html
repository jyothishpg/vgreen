From Quick Contracts to a Full Engineering Team — VGreenTEK's End-to-End Delivery for a leading Medical IT Services

A Trivandrum-based IT services company needed to move fast — starting with 3 contract developers and scaling into a 6-person full-time engineering team, all within five months.

ABOUT THE CLIENT

A technology services and consulting company delivering solutions across web, mobile, and cloud technologies. As a growing organization operating in a highly competitive talent market, they required a trusted hiring partner capable of rapidly sourcing skilled engineers for both immediate project delivery and long-term team expansion.

Client: DeviceDriven

Location: Trivandrum, Kerala, India

IndustryIT Services & Consulting

OUR INTERVENTION

  • VGreenTEK partnered with DeviceDriven across two strategic hiring tracks.
  • The first focused on rapid contract staffing, delivering three specialist developers within a 28-day window to support active client projects and ensure uninterrupted delivery.
  • The second involved a structured full-time hiring initiative spanning five months, helping DeviceDriven build a robust engineering team across cloud, infrastructure, mobile, backend, and architectural disciplines.
  • By managing both tracks simultaneously, VGreenTEK enabled DeviceDriven to maintain project momentum while building a scalable and future-ready engineering organization.

IMPACT

Rapid Contract Deployment

Delivered 3 contract developers within 28 days to support immediate project requirements.

Complete Team Buildout

Successfully placed 6 full-time engineers across critical technical functions.

Diverse Technology Coverage

Supported hiring across Java, Fullstack, NodeJS, DevOps, SRE, Database Administration, React Native, and Solution Architecture.

Scalable Talent Strategy

Enabled DeviceDriven to transition smoothly from short-term project support to long-term capability building.

Faster Business Growth

Provided the engineering capacity needed to support ongoing delivery while preparing for future expansion.
