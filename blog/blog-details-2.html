Augmenting Concora Credit's Cloud Engineering Team with Staff Augmentation Mode

ABOUT THE CLIENT

Concora Credit (formerly Genesis Financial Solutions) is a leading US-based non-prime consumer financing company serving millions of customers through products including the Milestone, Indigo, Destiny, and Earniva Mastercards.

With revenues of approximately $346M and a cloud-first technology ecosystem built on Microsoft Azure, the company operates at significant scale, requiring highly reliable cloud infrastructure and senior engineering expertise.

Client: Concora Credit Inc.

Location: Trivandrum, Kerala, India

Founded: 2001

Company Size500–1,000 Employees

OUR INTERVENTION

  • Concora Credit needed to strengthen its cloud infrastructure and service reliability using Azure Reliability Engineering, DevOps Automation, and Infrastructure-as-Code while maintaining critical 24x7 support.
  • VGreenTEK addressed this challenge through a targeted hiring-based staff augmentation strategy, sourcing senior-level contractors with proven Azure expertise and deploying them specifically for the requirements agreed throughout the engagement.
  • By integrating directly into Concora Credit's hiring workflow, VGreenTEK ensured every position was filled with precision, delivering real infrastructure value to a highly regulated fintech operating at national scale.

IMPACT

Cost-Effective Talent Strategy
