ABOUT THE CLIENT

DigitRell, a business unit of JoeNJack Touch Pvt. Ltd., is a technology consulting and IT services company helping organizations navigate complexity through enterprise technology, SAP solutions, AI-driven services, and digital transformation initiatives.

With operations spanning multiple countries and industries, DigitRell required a scalable hiring strategy to support its rapid growth and expanding client engagements.

Client: DigitRell

Location: Kochi, Kerala, India

Presence:4+ Countries | 10+Sectos | 75+ Projects

OUR INTERVENTION

  • As DigitRell accelerated its growth trajectory, the company needed skilled technology professionals across multiple domains and geographies.
  • VGreenTEK partnered with DigitRell through a structured multi-phase hiring strategy, managing both full-time recruitment and contract staffing requirements simultaneously. Over six months, our team delivered a continuous stream of qualified professionals across India and the USA, ensuring project momentum and business growth remained uninterrupted.
  • By maintaining a consistent hiring cadence, VGreenTEK helped DigitRell strengthen its delivery capabilities, expand technical expertise, and meet evolving client demands without delays.
