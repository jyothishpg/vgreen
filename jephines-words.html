Founders Story

Built with intention
DDriven by depth.

We started VGreenTEK because we knew exactly what good looked like — and we wanted to build it. Not from the outside, but from the ground up.

FOUNDER’S VOICE

In Jephin's words

Co-founder & COO — on purpose, people, and building VGreenTEK from the ground up.

Jephin Jose

Co-founder & COO, VGreenTEK · Kochi, India

01 — Problem

What problem do you care most about solving?

Talent misplacement. The world isn't short of talent — it's short of people who know how to find it in unconventional places. That gap is what we exist to close.

02 — Belief

What belief do you hold that most people disagree with? I believe hiring is still fundamentally a people business, despite the rise of AI and automation. While technology can improve efficiency, strong relationships, trust, and human judgment remain the most important factors in making successful placements

03 — Origin

Why start VGreenTEK instead of joining something else? I saw a gap between great talent and the right opportunities, and I wanted to build a company focused on creating lasting value for both clients and candidates. Rather than joining an existing firm, I wanted the freedom to build a business driven by trust, quality, and long-term relationships.

04 — Values

What principles do you never compromise on? Transparency with clients, even when it's uncomfortable. Every hard decision comes back to one question: will the people involved still trust us when this is over?

05 — Vision

What does success look like beyond revenue? A placed candidate calling us five years later to say that role changed their trajectory. And proving that a firm built in Kochi can operate at global standards — not by chasing scale, but by never cutting corners.

06 — Learning

A failure that fundamentally changed how you lead? A placement that failed because I focused on the role spec and missed the team dynamics. The job description is only half the brief. That lesson cost us a client. It made us a better firm.

07 — To clients

One thing you'd tell every customer? Tell us what you actually need, not what you think you can ask for. We work best as partners, not vendors. The more honest you are with us, the better we serve you.

08 — Culture

What do you want employees to say in five years? That That they were trusted before they were ready. That VGreenTEK gave them real responsibility and saw their potential before they did.

09 — Influence

What has shaped your thinking most? The engine room of a VLCC vessel. When you're responsible for machinery keeping 300,000 tonnes moving, you learn that systems matter and complacency kills. That's stayed with me in every role since.

10 — The future

What principles do you never compromise on? Depth over breadth, and build trust before they build scale for both our clients and candidates. Long-term trust is far more valuable than any short-term gain.

"The future will belong to those who choose depth over breadth, and build trust before they build scale."

— Jephin Jose, Co-founder & COO, VGreenTEK
